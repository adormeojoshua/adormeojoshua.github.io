Projects

Thesis Project

CATS, a search and rescue robot

A robotic car that looks for people in places that are hard to reach. It drives from a phone, streams a live camera feed, and flags humans and animals with on-board object detection.

  • Raspberry Pi runs YOLOv8 and OpenCV for real-time detection on the camera stream
  • ESP32 drives the motors from UDP commands and stops them if the signal drops
  • Android app for driving, camera view, two-way audio, and photo capture

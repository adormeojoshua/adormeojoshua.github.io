Thesis project

CATS, a search and rescue robot

A robotic car that looks for people in places that are hard to reach. It drives from a phone, streams a live camera feed, and flags humans and animals with on-board object detection.

  • Raspberry Pi runs YOLOv8 and OpenCV for real-time detection on the camera stream
  • ESP32 drives the motors from UDP commands and stops them if the signal drops
  • Android app for driving, camera view, two-way audio, and photo capture
  • LiDAR and a gas sensor on board

Academic project

DonasyON, giving in one tap

A Philippine donation app that turns giving into a habit. Donors earn points, climb a leaderboard, and redeem rewards, while verified users can start their own campaigns.

  • SwiftUI interface designed in Figma and built with MVVM
  • Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for accounts and live campaign data
  • Dual-point system, weekly and all-time leaderboard, and a rewards store with voucher codes
  • ID and selfie verification flow before anyone can publish a campaign
  • Payments and verification are simulated for the course
